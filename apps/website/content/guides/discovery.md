# Discover people and releases

Follow the people, studios, and upcoming releases connected to works in the catalog.

## People and studios

Open either directory to look up a name or alias. Both directories show entries with and without artwork by default. Use the artwork filter to narrow the list. Sort by name or by public work count, then open a record to follow its credits.

## The release calendar

The calendar brings together films, season starts, and registered episodes. Use the monthly calendar for dates, the timeline for sequence, or the upcoming view for what is ahead.
