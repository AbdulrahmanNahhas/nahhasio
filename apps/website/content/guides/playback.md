# Playback

Watch brings the video, episode navigation, work information, and family discussion together.

## Pick the episode

On a series, use the episode list to search, change ordering, or switch between all and unwatched episodes. For a film, the video uses the available width and the episode sidebar is hidden.

## Audio and subtitles

Choose from the tracks available on the current source. A title’s catalog language labels describe the work and its installments. The player uses the actual tracks available for the selected video.

## Save a video for later

Use **Download to device** when you want offline playback. Read [Save and watch offline](#docs/offline) for the difference between retaining a video and saving its catalog entry.
