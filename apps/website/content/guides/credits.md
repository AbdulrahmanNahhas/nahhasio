# Titles and dubbing

A title entry brings together artwork, descriptions, credits, seasons, and episodes. You can catalog a title before you have the video.

## Arabic dubbing credits

Dubbing information belongs to the work. When contributions are recorded, they can connect the Arabic production to the people and studios behind it.
