# Artwork and editorial details

Artwork, cast and crew, credits, awards, and notes give the collection context beyond a list of files.

## Images at the right level

Posters, banners, and logos support the different Browse layouts. An episode still belongs to its episode, so it can appear alongside the corresponding episode title and details.

## Scores and notes

Browse can filter by the overall rating and by individual scoring criteria. Where scores and notes are available, the work and installment pages give more detail.

## Awards and credits

The catalog can associate credits with people and studios, and record recognition with award organizations, categories, and ceremonies.
